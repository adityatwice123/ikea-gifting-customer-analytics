# BRD Summary

Full document: [IKEA_Gifting_BRD.docx](IKEA_Gifting_BRD.docx).

**Objective:** find the most valuable customer segments and measure churn risk so marketing and CRM teams can target effort instead of treating everyone the same.

**Questions:** who are the most valuable customers; who has gone quiet; how value and retention trend over time; how this differs by country; what to do for each segment.

**In scope:** SQL cleaning, Python RFM segmentation, 90 day churn flag, three page Power BI dashboard.
**Out of scope:** predictive churn models, product recommendations, live scoring, running campaigns.

**Definitions:** Recency is days since last purchase. Frequency is distinct invoices. Monetary is total revenue. Churn is no purchase in the trailing 90 days.
